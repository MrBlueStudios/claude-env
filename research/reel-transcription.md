# reel-transcription
**Question:** How does a public Instagram reel or TikTok video's audio become text on this NixOS laptop, free, with yt-dlp and faster-whisper on CPU?
**Answer:** Use `yt-dlp -x` to fetch the audio, then faster-whisper (int8, CPU) with the `small` or `large-v3-turbo` model. All of it is in nixpkgs on both 25.05 and 26.05, and a 30-90 s reel should take seconds to tens of seconds. TikTok videos usually work without login. Instagram is the fragile part: it is increasingly login-walled and rate-limited, and datacenter IPs (Contabo) are likely to fare worse than the laptop. TikTok photo slideshows give audio only (via `/photo/` to `/video/`), and Instagram image carousels are not really supported.
**Confidence:** medium, because the nixpkgs versions and yt-dlp behaviour are verified, but the reel timings are extrapolated from one published benchmark and nothing was run on this machine.
**What would change our decision:**
- If Instagram reels fail without cookies on the laptop too, we would need a throwaway-account cookies file, or we would drop Instagram support.
- If the real-world speed on the Arc iGPU matters, whisper.cpp or OpenVINO becomes worth a look. For 30-90 s clips CPU is probably already fast enough.

## Findings
1. **yt-dlp in nixpkgs.** The attribute is `pkgs.yt-dlp` (file `pkgs/by-name/yt/yt-dlp/package.nix`). It is at 2025.11.12 on 25.05 and 2026.10.26 on 26.05. The 25.05 version is about a year old, which matters because Instagram and TikTok extractors break often. Plan to override or pull yt-dlp from unstable or nightly on the 25.05 machine. Sources: [25.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-25.05/pkgs/by-name/yt/yt-dlp/package.nix), [26.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-26.05/pkgs/by-name/yt/yt-dlp/package.nix).
2. **faster-whisper in nixpkgs.** `python3Packages.faster-whisper` is 1.1.1 on 25.05 and 1.2.1 on 26.05. Sources: [25.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-25.05/pkgs/development/python-modules/faster-whisper/default.nix), [26.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-26.05/pkgs/development/python-modules/faster-whisper/default.nix).
3. **CLI wrapper.** `whisper-ctranslate2` is in nixpkgs under `pkgs/by-name/wh/whisper-ctranslate2` at 0.5.2 on 25.05 and 0.5.7 on 26.05. It is a drop-in CLI over faster-whisper, so it is the least code for us. Sources: [25.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-25.05/pkgs/by-name/wh/whisper-ctranslate2/package.nix), [26.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-26.05/pkgs/by-name/wh/whisper-ctranslate2/package.nix). `wyoming-faster-whisper`: UNVERIFIED. The guessed nixpkgs path returned 404. It is a Home Assistant voice server and not what we need anyway.
4. **whisper-cpp alternative.** `pkgs.whisper-cpp` is 1.7.5 on 25.05 and 1.8.4 on 26.05. It has `vulkanSupport` (off by default), `cudaSupport` and `rocmSupport` flags. It has no OpenVINO and no SYCL option. Sources: [25.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-25.05/pkgs/by-name/wh/whisper-cpp/package.nix), [26.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/nixos-26.05/pkgs/by-name/wh/whisper-cpp/package.nix). Whether Vulkan on Arc actually beats CPU for short clips: UNVERIFIED, no benchmark found.
5. **Speed benchmark.** The faster-whisper README benchmark has `small` with int8 on CPU transcribing 13 minutes of audio in 1 min 42 s. The CPU is an i7-12700K with 8 threads and beam size 5, using about 1.5 GB RAM. That is about 7.6x real time. The same README lists whisper.cpp at 2m05s on the same test. These figures were seen second-hand, so they are secondary. Source: [faster-whisper README via fork](https://github.com/AIXerum/faster-whisper).
6. **Extrapolation for a 60 s reel (UNVERIFIED, not measured here).** Scaling the figure above, `small` int8 should take about 8 s on that CPU; the Core Ultra 9 285H with 16 threads should be similar or better. `tiny` and `base` should take roughly 1-4 s. `medium` should take roughly 25-40 s. `large-v3-turbo` should take roughly 15-30 s (it has a decoder with 4 layers instead of 32, so it is far faster than `large-v3`). Add 2-10 s for model load on each run. One report measures large-v3-turbo on CPU int8 at 7.7 min for a long file, which fits this scale. Source for that figure: [TalkTrack issue #131](https://github.com/ObscureAintSecure/TalkTrack/issues/131).
7. **Model download sizes (UNVERIFIED, from memory, not checked against Hugging Face).** Roughly tiny 75 MB, base 145 MB, small 480 MB, medium 1.5 GB, large-v3-turbo 1.6 GB. They are fetched from Hugging Face on first use.
8. **Intel Arc and CTranslate2.** CTranslate2 has no Intel GPU backend. It supports CPU and NVIDIA CUDA only. Issue #2032 (oneAPI/SYCL request) is still open, and a Sep 2026 comment describes only a proof of concept with no speedup. So faster-whisper cannot use the Arc iGPU. Sources: [CTranslate2 #2032](https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2/issues/2032), [#1715](https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2/issues/1715).
9. **OpenVINO route.** A TalkTrack issue measured large-v3-turbo through OpenVINO GenAI on a Core Ultra iGPU at 54 s versus 7.7 min on CPU int8, roughly 8x faster. That is a user report, not a controlled benchmark, and nixpkgs `whisper-cpp` does not enable OpenVINO. Source: [TalkTrack #131](https://github.com/ObscureAintSecure/TalkTrack/issues/131). For 30-90 s clips it is not worth it; CPU is fast enough.
10. **Instagram needs login today?** Often, yes. yt-dlp commonly errors with "Requested content is not available, rate-limit reached or login required." Public reels can work signed out until Instagram's anonymous rate limit pushes you to a login wall. In issue #17124 a commenter said no-cookie downloads had just stopped working. That issue was tested on stable 2026.06.09 and nightly 2026.07.02, and it also shows cookies do not always fix it. Username/password login does not work; only cookies do. Sources: [#17124](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/17124), [#16311](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/16311), [#14241](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/14241).
11. **Cookie risk.** A yt-dlp maintainer warned in #7873 that Instagram detects unofficial clients and bans accounts "without hesitation." Any logged-in method carries ban risk, so use a throwaway account, never the real one. Chrome cookie extraction is reported broken by app-bound encryption; Firefox works (this claim is from a guide, so medium confidence). Stories fail even with Firefox cookies (October 2026 reports, [#17903](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/17903), [#17918](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/17918)). Source for the maintainer warning: [#7873](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/7873).
12. **TikTok.** No evidence found that normal TikTok video URLs need login. Not tested, so UNVERIFIED. TikTok extractors do break and get fixed fairly often, so keep yt-dlp current.
13. **TikTok photo slideshows.** A `/photo/` URL fails with "Unsupported URL". A contributor in [#17865](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/17865) (closed Oct 2026 as a duplicate of #15764) said photos are out of scope, and rewriting `/photo/` to `/video/` downloads only the audio track. For transcription that audio is just the background music, so there is usually nothing to transcribe. gallery-dl is the tool that gets the images.
14. **Instagram carousels.** yt-dlp treats a carousel as a playlist. Video items work. Image items fail with "No video formats found!", which can abort the whole run unless you pass `--ignore-no-formats-error`. Multi-video posts download all items, even with `?img_index=`. A maintainer said yt-dlp is not an image downloader. Sources: [#7569](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/7569), [#11792](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/11792), [#12983](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/12983), [#17133](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/17133).
15. **Contabo VPS.** The compute side is fine. CTranslate2 int8 on 4-8 vCPU should handle a reel in tens of seconds with `small`. The download side is the problem. Datacenter IPs are widely treated as bot traffic by large platforms. Evidence for Instagram specifically is thin: [#7873](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/7873) is a 2023 VPS report and the rest found is YouTube-focused. Whether Contabo ranges are blocked by Instagram today is UNVERIFIED. The practical design is to download on the laptop or a residential connection and transcribe wherever. Sources: [yt-dlp on a server](https://ytdlp.org/guides/run-yt-dlp-server), [dev.to](https://dev.to/yyildiz/yt-dlp-works-on-my-laptop-but-not-on-my-server-heres-whats-actually-going-on-2a75).
16. **Terms of service.** UNVERIFIED. The fetch of Instagram's terms returned only a page heading, and TikTok's terms were not retrieved. From general knowledge, both prohibit automated collection without permission, and Instagram's terms say you may not access it "in an automated way" without permission. Technically this is a terms breach, not a crime. The realistic consequence for low-volume personal use is a rate limit, an IP block or a flagged account (if cookies are used), not legal action. Read the current terms before relying on this.

## Not checked
- Nothing was run on this machine, so there are no measured timings, model sizes or real Instagram or TikTok test downloads (research-only role, no execution tooling).
- The TikTok terms and Instagram terms text were not fetched.
- Whether Contabo IP ranges are currently blocked by Instagram or TikTok was not checked.
- No whisper.cpp Vulkan-on-Arc benchmarks were looked for, and whether `wyoming-faster-whisper` is packaged was not tested.
- gallery-dl was not evaluated as a nixpkgs package, though it is the obvious fallback for slideshows and image carousels.
